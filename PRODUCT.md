# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and energy analysts exploring European renewable generation. They open the app to monitor live/historical ENTSO-E data, compare countries, and view per-country dashboards—often on a laptop during demos or coursework.

## Product Purpose

EU Renewables Monitor surfaces live and historical renewable electricity data across Europe (map, dashboards, comparison). Success is clear, trustworthy energy data with fast navigation between overview → map → country detail.

## Brand Personality

Calm · Credible · Technical-green

Corporate Enevo-adjacent: navy/indigo rails, forest green data accents, teal interactive highlights. Confident ops tool, not a marketing splash site.

## Anti-references

- Neon “AI dark mode” glow UIs
- Generic purple SaaS gradients
- Heavy card stacks and pill clutter on auth screens
- Playful consumer eco-mascot aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product** — Auth should feel like entering the monitor, not a separate marketing site.
2. **Data clarity first** — Surfaces and type stay readable; glass effects never wash out labels or errors.
3. **Brand continuity** — Same navy/teal/forest language as sidebar and home hero.
4. **Quiet motion** — Short ease-out reveals; respect reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast on form text and controls. Support `prefers-reduced-motion`. Password fields keep visible show/hide controls with clear labels.
